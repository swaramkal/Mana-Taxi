Frequently Asked Questions

Is booking free?

Yes. Customers can submit ride bookings without a booking fee or confirmation payment.

Which vehicles are available?

MANA TAXI supports Mini, Sedan and SUV categories.

How can I register as a driver?

Select Drive with us or Register as a Driver. Your registration requires approval before receiving rides.

Can I cancel a ride?

Ride cancellation is available according to the booking status and platform cancellation rules.

How are fares calculated?

The platform displays estimated fares based on configured rates and route distance. Actual fares depend on the final fare policy.
